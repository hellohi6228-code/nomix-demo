Privacy Policy

Nomix · Last updated November 12, 2026

Nomix operates restaurant brands, including MatchaZen. This policy explains how the Nomix marketing app ("the app") handles information. The app is used only by Nomix staff to manage the social media accounts of Nomix's own restaurants.

What we collect

  • Staff accounts: name, email address and language preference of Nomix staff who sign in.
  • Connected social accounts: when a Nomix account owner connects Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or a Google Business Profile, we receive the account's ID, username and an access token that lets us publish content and read messages and reviews for that account.
  • Guest messages and reviews: messages, comments and reviews sent to Nomix restaurants, including the sender's public name or username.
  • Content: photos, videos and text uploaded by staff for posts.

How we use it

  • To draft, schedule and publish posts that Nomix staff have approved.
  • To reply to guest messages and reviews on behalf of Nomix restaurants.
  • To show staff the status of their accounts and content.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising to individuals.

TikTok

With TikTok we use only the access the account owner grants (basic profile information, video upload and video publishing) to publish videos that Nomix staff approved to that account. The account owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok's settings or in the app.

Storage and security

Information is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) in the United States. Access tokens are encrypted and are never shown in the app. Access to data is limited by role.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep information while the account is connected or as needed to operate the restaurants' marketing. To ask for your information to be deleted, or to disconnect an account, email us.
